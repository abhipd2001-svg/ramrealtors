Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This page describes how Ram Realtors handles information you choose to provide through this website. It is general website information, not legal advice. Please review and update it to reflect the business’s actual practices before publication.

Information you provide

Inquiry forms may ask for your name, phone number, optional email address, property locality, property preferences or details, budget, and message. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive personal information through these forms.

Why it is used

The information is used to understand and respond to your property inquiry and, where relevant, to follow up about that request. It should not be used for unrelated purposes.

Form and messaging providers

When deployed with Netlify Forms, form submissions are processed by Netlify under its own policies and service terms. If you choose to contact the business using WhatsApp, information you share is also processed by WhatsApp under its policies. Review the relevant provider policies before submitting information.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you submitted, use the contact options on the website when available.

Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website or its information-handling practices. Edit this page if the business’s actual practices change.
